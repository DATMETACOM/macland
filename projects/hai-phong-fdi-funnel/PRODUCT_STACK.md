# Product Stack

## What We Are Actually Selling

Do not think only in terms of "listing."

You need three layers of product:

1. physical supply products
2. advisory / transaction products
3. trust products

## A. Physical Supply Products

These are the tangible things the customer can rent, acquire, or shortlist.

## 1. Ready-built factories

Best for fastest early deals.

**Observed Hai Phong examples in the current repo**

- factory around Dinh Vu Industrial Park
- factory around An Lao
- factory around An Duong Industrial Park
- factory around Canh Hau Industrial Park
- factory around Trang Due area

**Best fit customers**

- Korean electronics suppliers
- Taiwanese / Chinese supporting-industry firms
- Japanese SMEs that want lower setup friction

## 2. Industrial land in established parks

Best for stronger investors who want to self-build or secure a long-term base.

**Observed Hai Phong examples in the current repo**

- DEEP C / Dinh Vu-related land
- Trang Due-related land
- VSIP Hai Phong-related land
- Nam Cau Kien-related land
- Japan Industrial Park / Nomura-related land
- An Duong-related land

**Best fit customers**

- Japanese manufacturers
- Korean groups with long-term expansion plans
- larger Taiwanese / Chinese manufacturers

## 3. Upcoming industrial park opportunities

Useful, but not the first commercial spearhead unless supply is very well controlled.

**Observed Hai Phong examples in the current repo**

- Trang Due 3
- Tien Thanh
- Nam Trang Cat
- Nomura phase 2
- Southern coastal and airport-related park concepts
- multiple upcoming industrial clusters

**Best fit customers**

- land-banking investors
- groups with long planning horizons
- developers and strategic entrants

## B. Advisory / Transaction Products

These are sellable service packages, not free extras.

## 1. Site shortlist package

You deliver:

- 3-5 matched options
- comparison table
- pros / cons
- infrastructure and legal snapshot
- recommendation

This is the easiest service product to sell early.

## 2. Site visit package

You deliver:

- agenda
- scheduling
- route planning
- translation / interpretation
- site comparison notes
- next-step memo

This works for both online-origin and offline-origin leads.

## 3. Negotiation support package

You deliver:

- landlord coordination
- commercial comparison
- issue tracking
- concession management

## 4. Market-entry support package

You deliver:

- basic setup guidance
- local partner introductions
- licensing / legal coordination via partners
- design-build / utilities / recruitment introductions

## C. Trust Products

These are not separate invoices first. They are proof assets that make sales easier.

## 1. Verified asset dossier

For each serious listing:

- title summary
- zoning / use fit
- area
- current status
- utilities
- wastewater
- firefighting
- truck/container access
- handover timeline
- asking price / fee structure
- owner seriousness

## 2. Investor memo

For each serious lead:

- customer requirement summary
- why Hai Phong
- why this park / site
- risk notes
- next actions

## 3. Market-specific landing story

- Korean version
- Japanese version
- Chinese version
- English version

These are lighter than a full site rebuild and should support outreach.

For the first 60-90 days, sell this combined offer:

"Verified Hai Phong factory shortlist + site visit + negotiation support"

That is easier to close than a vague "industrial investment consulting" promise.
