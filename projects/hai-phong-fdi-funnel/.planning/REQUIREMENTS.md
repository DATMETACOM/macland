# Requirements: Hai Phong FDI Funnel

**Defined:** 2026-04-11
**Core Value:** Reduce uncertainty for foreign industrial investors by turning fragmented Hai Phong supply into verified, decision-ready opportunities with strong local execution support.

## v1 Requirements

### Positioning

- [ ] **POS-01**: Project defines one primary customer wedge for the first commercial push.
- [ ] **POS-02**: Project defines one primary asset type for the first commercial push.
- [ ] **POS-03**: Project defines a clear value proposition that is stronger than a generic industrial broker or listing site.

### Supply

- [ ] **SUP-01**: Team has a verification checklist for factory and industrial-land opportunities in Hai Phong.
- [ ] **SUP-02**: Team defines a minimum data-room standard for each qualified opportunity.
- [ ] **SUP-03**: Team distinguishes verified, semi-verified, and unverified supply.
- [ ] **SUP-04**: Team defines an exclusivity or privileged-access strategy for the most valuable inventory.

### Demand

- [ ] **DEM-01**: Team defines separate messaging for Korea, Japan, China/Taiwan, EU, and US targets.
- [ ] **DEM-02**: Team defines first-wave outbound and inbound channels for the primary wedge.
- [ ] **DEM-03**: Team defines lead-capture and qualification questions tied to actual deal readiness.
- [ ] **DEM-04**: Team defines a response SLA and first-contact playbook for investor leads.

### Deal Operations

- [ ] **OPS-01**: Team defines a CRM pipeline from inquiry to close.
- [ ] **OPS-02**: Team defines stage gates for shortlist, site visit, commercial discussion, and close.
- [ ] **OPS-03**: Team defines owner responsibilities for each deal stage.
- [ ] **OPS-04**: Team defines a standard investor memo and landlord comparison sheet.

### Proof

- [ ] **PRF-01**: Team defines what counts as first proof of demand.
- [ ] **PRF-02**: Team defines target metrics for first qualified leads, site visits, and signed deals.
- [ ] **PRF-03**: Team defines a case-study capture process for closed deals.

## v2 Requirements

### Productization

- **PROD-01**: Build a dedicated multilingual investor-facing site or microsite for the validated wedge.
- **PROD-02**: Add automated lead scoring and CRM integration.
- **PROD-03**: Add a partner portal for landlords, industrial park operators, and service partners.

### Expansion

- **EXPN-01**: Expand from Hai Phong into adjacent northern industrial markets only after Hai Phong motion works.
- **EXPN-02**: Add second-wave country-specific funnels after first-wave deals are validated.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Broad Vietnam-wide inventory strategy from day one | Too wide for a trust-heavy and operationally complex offer |
| Building a media/news business | Does not directly solve investor uncertainty or close deals |
| Large software build before manual validation | Risks scaling assumptions instead of learning from customers |
