# Hai Phong Context Notes

## Internal Website Context

Observed from the current repo:

- The site is an industrial real-estate listing platform built with Next.js.
- Hai Phong products are prioritized in the product ordering logic.
- Contact capture is basic email-based lead forwarding, not a full qualification workflow.
- The current dataset contains 408 products, with 62 Hai Phong-related listings.
- Hai Phong supply appears skewed toward transfer and upcoming opportunities, which implies a likely trust and verification burden for serious foreign investors.

## Paul Graham Lens

From "How to Start a Startup":

- make something customers actually want
- good people matter more than a brilliant starting idea
- early learning should come from direct contact with customers

Applied here:

- the core customer is not "people browsing industrial real estate"
- the core customer is a foreign manufacturer or investor trying to reduce location and execution risk
- therefore the startup should optimize for validated investor pain, not listing volume

## Source-Backed External Context

### Hai Phong growth and policy

- Hai Phong Portal reported 2024 GRDP growth of 11.01%, ranking first in the Red River Delta and first among centrally-run cities.
- The same city source frames 2025 around expanding economic and urban space, special mechanisms, and green/digital transformation.

### FDI momentum

- HEZA states that total FDI attracted in 2024 reached about US$4.909 billion.
- HEZA also highlights a 2025 investment-promotion conference associated with roughly US$15.5 billion in certificates and MOUs.

### Industrial expansion

- Tien Thanh Industrial Park broke ground on 2025-04-20 with about 410.46 hectares and nearly VND4,600 billion total investment.
- HEZA materials also point to continued industrial park expansion and large-scale land supply across key parks.

### Logistics and infrastructure

- HEZA and government sources emphasize the Hanoi-Hai Phong-Quang Ninh corridor, coastal expressway, Tan Vu-Lach Huyen connection, and Lach Huyen deep-water port.
- Government and HEZA materials position Lach Huyen as a strategic port asset linked to direct long-haul export routes and future logistics growth.
- Government reporting in December 2025 also tied the Lao Cai-Ha Noi-Hai Phong railway project to Lach Huyen seaport connectivity.

### International investor fit

- HEZA materials show active Korean, Japanese, Chinese/Taiwanese, and APEC-facing investor engagement.
- Korean electronics presence is materially proven by LG Display investment in Hai Phong.
- Japanese investor relevance is reinforced by Nomura/Japanese Industrial Park history and HEZA outreach with Gunma and Japanese enterprises.

## Working Inference

Hai Phong's strength is not just that it is "good for industry." Its stronger commercial story is:

- export-capable northern manufacturing base
- strong policy attention
- port-centered logistics
- expanding industrial land and park supply
- proven FDI ecosystems in sectors relevant to East Asian manufacturers

That suggests the commercial wedge should be narrower than "all foreign investors" and more concrete than "industrial real estate information."
