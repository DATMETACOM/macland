## Key Findings

- Hai Phong is macro-attractive, but the business will fail if it behaves like a generic portal.
- The strongest near-term bet is a services-led, trust-heavy, verified-supply model.
- The current website already proves supply presence, but not a mature international funnel.
- The likely best first markets are Korea, Japan, and Greater China.
- For mainland-China outreach under current constraints, the highest-probability path is referral + named account targeting + Chinese-language sales assets, not Douyin-first execution.

## Immediate Next Step

  first 50 target accounts, first 10 verified opportunities, first Chinese/Vietnamese/English sales assets, and first outreach wave.

## Risks To Watch

- Over-scoping across too many countries at once
- Confusing content production with commercial traction
- Using weak or stale supply
- Underestimating the operational nature of cross-border deals
