# Execution Order

## Short Answer

Do not choose between `hồ sơ năng lực` and `storytelling` as if only one should exist.

For this business, the correct early sequence is:

1. mini hồ sơ năng lực with real proof
2. productized offer sheet
3. 3-5 sharp market stories
4. target-account outreach

## Why

This is a high-ticket, trust-heavy sale.

If you lead with story only, you look soft.
If you lead with deck only, you look generic.

You need proof first, then narrative that makes the proof memorable.

## What The First Version Should Look Like

## 1. Mini hồ sơ năng lực

Not a huge corporate brochure.

Just a tight 8-12 page deck:

- who you are
- why Hai Phong
- what supply you can open
- how your process works
- what problems you solve for foreign investors
- what parks / asset types you cover
- what support you provide beyond brokerage
- what proof you already have

## 2. Offer sheet

One page per sellable offer:

- verified factory shortlist
- industrial land shortlist
- site visit support
- negotiation support

## 3. Story assets

Not broad brand storytelling.

Use targeted stories such as:

- "Why Korean electronics suppliers should look at Hai Phong now"
- "Fast move-in factory options near Hai Phong logistics corridors"
- "How to compare ready-built factory vs land-for-build in Hai Phong"

## 4. Outreach list

Once the above exists, you can run:

- LinkedIn
- email
- referral introductions
- Zalo/WeChat/LINE where relevant
- offline meetings with a consistent message

## Can You Borrow Analysis From Successful Cases?

Yes, but do it honestly.

Borrow:

- positioning logic
- trust-building structure
- investor-service model
- one-stop-service framing
- testimonial and proof architecture
- market-segment-specific messaging

Do not borrow:

- fake case studies
- fake transaction numbers
- invented investor logos

## Useful Patterns From Public Cases

### VSIP pattern

- one-stop service
- pre-licensing and post-licensing support
- operational support around the industrial product

### DEEP C pattern

- clear productization: land, ready-built workshop/warehouse, utilities, services
- multilingual market-facing presentation
- testimonials and client quotes
- sustainability / eco-industrial positioning

### HEZA / city pattern

- use ecosystem proof:
  investors, policy support, logistics, labor, infrastructure

## Recommendation For You

Because you want early flow, do this first:

- mini hồ sơ năng lực
- top 10 verified Hai Phong opportunities
- 3 market stories
- first 50 named target accounts

That is enough to start both online and offline selling.
