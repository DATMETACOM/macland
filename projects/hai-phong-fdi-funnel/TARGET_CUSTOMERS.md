# Target Customers

## Principle

Do not start from "all foreign investors."

Start from investors who already fit Hai Phong's actual strengths:

- export manufacturing
- electronics and supporting industries
- port-linked production and logistics
- suppliers following anchor investors
- occupiers who need speed and lower setup risk

## Priority Tiers

## Tier 1: Fastest Path To Real Deals

### 1. Korean electronics and component manufacturers

**Why this tier matters**

- Hai Phong already has strong Korean industrial presence, especially around LG and related ecosystems.
- Korea is one of the clearest proven investor pools in Hai Phong.
- These firms often value speed, operating reliability, and proximity to existing clusters.

**Best fit products**

- ready-built factories from 3,000-12,000 sqm
- transfer-ready factories with legal clarity
- industrial land near established electronics ecosystems

**Signals of high intent**

- supplier to LG, LG Innotek, or Korean electronics clusters
- current factory in Bac Ninh/Bac Giang/Hai Duong is full or constrained
- customer asks early about utilities, worker availability, or move-in timeline

### 2. Japanese supporting-industry manufacturers

**Why this tier matters**

- Hai Phong already has Japan Industrial Park / Nomura and a known Japanese operating environment.
- Japanese manufacturers usually value stability, compliance, and long-term partner trust.

**Best fit products**

- high-quality industrial land in proven parks
- ready-built factory with strong legal and technical documents
- build-to-suit options with disciplined local support

**Signals of high intent**

- supplier to Japanese OEMs or Tier-1 suppliers
- interest in a long lease horizon
- need for predictable regulatory and operating environment

### 3. Taiwanese and Chinese supporting-industry manufacturers

**Why this tier matters**

- HEZA explicitly promotes cooperation with Pegatron and Taiwanese supporting-industry enterprises.
- This segment often moves faster than EU/US and is highly relevant to relocation/diversification into Vietnam.

**Best fit products**

- ready-built factories for quick start
- land for self-build or staged expansion
- sites with strong port access and worker availability

**Signals of high intent**

- China-plus-one expansion
- supplier to Pegatron or global EMS chains
- decision-maker wants shortlisting fast, before deep branding conversations

## Tier 2: Good Market, Slower Motion

### 4. EU mid-market manufacturers

**Why this tier matters**

- Good fit for sustainable parks, better-quality infrastructure, and long-term planning.
- But documentation and diligence cycles are usually heavier.

**Best fit products**

- eco-industrial or sustainability-positioned parks
- build-to-suit land
- high-spec factory options

### 5. Logistics-linked industrial occupiers

**Why this tier matters**

- Hai Phong's port story is a real advantage.
- Some occupiers care more about seaport access and warehousing efficiency than cluster fit.

**Best fit products**

- Dinh Vu / DEEP C / port-adjacent factories or warehouses
- logistics-linked industrial land

## Tier 3: Strategic But Not First

### 6. US manufacturers and strategic investors

**Why this tier matters**

- Potentially high-value and strong branding impact.
- But likely slower, more selective, and more expensive to acquire at the start.

**Best fit products**

- high-quality land or build-to-suit
- ESG and compliance-ready factory solutions

## Ideal Customer Profiles

## ICP-1: Fast-Move Factory Tenant

- foreign manufacturer
- 3,000-12,000 sqm need
- wants move-in within 3-9 months
- prefers ready-built or near-ready factory
- values clear infrastructure and fast execution

## ICP-2: Expansion Land Investor

- existing manufacturer in North Vietnam or China/Taiwan/Korea/Japan
- needs 1-5 ha industrial land
- willing to build own facility or build-to-suit
- values logistics, utilities, and legal certainty

## ICP-3: Supplier-Following Entrant

- supplier following anchor clients such as LG, Pegatron, or related ecosystems
- needs to be near ecosystem nodes
- may begin with lease, then expand into land

## Target Account List Structure

For actual company-level outreach, build lists in this order:

1. suppliers already serving anchor manufacturers in or near Hai Phong
2. foreign firms already in North Vietnam but needing expansion
3. foreign firms in China/Taiwan/Korea/Japan publicly discussing Vietnam diversification
4. logistics and light-manufacturing occupiers with port-dependent operations

## Data Fields For Each Target Account

- company name
- country
- sector
- current factory location
- trigger event
- likely product fit
- target park / cluster
- decision-maker
- source link
- outreach status

## Immediate Recommendation

Build the first named target-account list around three pools:

- Korean electronics suppliers
- Japanese supporting-industry firms
- Taiwanese / Chinese suppliers linked to Pegatron-style ecosystems

That list should be much more actionable than trying to prospect the EU and US first.
