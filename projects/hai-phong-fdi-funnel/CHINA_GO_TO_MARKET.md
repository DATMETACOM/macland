# China Go-To-Market

## Executive View

If the goal is to close deals fast, do not make `Douyin` or `WeChat content operations` your first dependency.

Without a strong China-local operating setup, the highest-odds path is:

1. account-based prospecting
2. Chinese business associations already in Vietnam
3. target lists built from manufacturing expos and supply-chain events
4. referral-led introductions
5. email + LinkedIn for reachable decision-makers
6. only then: selective China-native media through a local partner or agency

## What Changes Because You Do Not Have A China ID

### What this blocks or weakens

- fast self-setup of some China-native content and ad operations
- smooth local verification or team operation on China-native social channels
- direct daily content execution inside the mainland ecosystem

### What it does NOT block

- building a China-target list
- outreach to Chinese manufacturers and suppliers
- using Vietnamese and Hai Phong Chinese business networks
- hosting Chinese-language landing pages on your own site
- sending email, PDFs, shortlists, and investor memos
- using exhibitions, chambers, and partner introductions

## Strategic Conclusion

For your current situation, `China acquisition` should be built as a business-development motion first, not a social-media motion first.

## China Customer Wedge

## Best first segment

- Chinese and Taiwanese manufacturers in electronics, supporting industry, machinery, packaging, plastics, light industrial components, and export manufacturing
- companies already in South China / East China and evaluating Vietnam capacity
- suppliers following anchor ecosystems such as LG / Pegatron / EMS / export manufacturers in North Vietnam

## Best first use cases

- ready-built factory for quick move-in
- 1-5 ha industrial land for self-build or build-to-suit
- relocation / overflow capacity / supplier-following expansion

## Segment Priority

### Priority 1

- Chinese enterprises already operating in Vietnam and looking for North Vietnam expansion
- Chinese enterprises already connected to Hai Phong through business associations or local parks

Why:

- easier access
- faster trust-building
- less cold-start friction

### Priority 2

- China-based manufacturers showing Vietnam expansion signals
- exhibitors / attendees from manufacturing and supply-chain events

Why:

- more scalable
- but colder and slower than in-country networks

### Priority 3

- broad China social acquisition through Douyin/WeChat content

Why:

- high effort
- lower near-term certainty
- operationally constrained in your current setup

## Channel Ranking For You

## Tier 1: Highest Probability

### 1. Chinese business associations in Vietnam

Why this matters:

- these are trust bridges, not just directories
- they reduce the "who are you?" problem
- they are closer to actual investors and operators than ad traffic

Useful nodes:

- Chinese Enterprises Association / China Chamber structures in Vietnam
- Hai Phong Chinese business community
- Hai Phong Taiwanese Chamber / Taiwan business community
- Hong Kong business association networks

Relevant signals from official/public sources:

- Hai Phong's 2025 foreign-community business program included the Chinese Business Association and the Taiwan Chamber of Commerce in Hai Phong.
- VCCI reported the Chinese Enterprises Association in Vietnam HCMC branch has around 500 members.

### 2. Warm introductions through parks, lawyers, EPCs, logistics, and accountants

Why this matters:

- foreign investors trust local commercial introductions more than ads
- industrial deals move through service networks

### 3. Expo- and exhibitor-based account lists

Best sources:

- CISCE
- NEPCON China
- electronica China
- sector-specific industrial fairs

Why:

- these events concentrate exactly the type of manufacturers you want
- they expose named companies, product categories, and industrial themes

## Tier 2: Strong Support Channels

### 4. Chinese-language landing pages on your own domain

What they should do:

- explain why Hai Phong
- show verified opportunities
- show process and trust assets
- support follow-up after introductions

These pages are for conversion support, not for organic China traffic at the start.

### 5. Email outreach

Use for:

- direct outreach to named accounts
- follow-up after events or referrals
- sending shortlist PDFs and investor memos

Do not use as:

- mass newsletter blast

### 6. LinkedIn

Use selectively:

- Chinese managers working internationally
- Hong Kong / Taiwan / Singapore / overseas-facing teams
- suppliers serving global OEMs

Do not assume it is your main mainland-China reach channel.

## Tier 3: Later

### 7. WeChat Official Account

This may be possible for overseas businesses through overseas setup routes according to operator guides, but it still requires localized operating capability and is not the fastest route to first deals.

Inference:

- useful later as a trust and follow-up channel
- not the first growth lever

### 8. Douyin

Low priority for your current stage.

Inference:

- it is content-heavy
- weak fit for a complex B2B industrial-location sale unless paired with a China-local operator and a serious media engine

### 9. Baidu Ads

Potentially useful later if you work through a qualified operator or distributor and have Chinese-language landing pages with a clear commercial offer.

But:

- this is not the first move
- it works better after your message and asset proof are sharper

## Winning Motion For China

## The right sequence

1. build Chinese-language credibility assets
2. build a named account list
3. get introductions through associations and local networks
4. send tailored shortlist / memo
5. move to call
6. move to site visit
7. move to negotiation

## The wrong sequence

1. open social channels
2. post general content
3. hope the right investor appears

## What You Need To Build First

### 1. Chinese one-page company deck

Must include:

- who you are
- why Hai Phong
- what types of assets you can access
- your support process
- what makes your offer lower risk

### 2. Chinese opportunity brief template

For each serious asset:

- location
- land / factory area
- utility status
- wastewater / EIA / PCCC status
- logistics distances
- handover timing
- fit industries
- commercial structure

### 3. Chinese investor memo

For each serious lead:

- customer requirement summary
- top 3 recommended options
- comparison
- key risks
- next actions

### 4. China account list

Build named accounts from:

- chambers and associations
- exhibitions
- supplier ecosystems
- companies already in Vietnam

## China Messaging Angles

### Angle 1: Speed

"Fast factory launch in Hai Phong with verified options and local execution support."

### Angle 2: Supply-chain fit

"Hai Phong as a port-linked manufacturing base for export and supplier-following expansion."

### Angle 3: Lower uncertainty

"We reduce site-selection risk by verifying real opportunities and coordinating local execution."

## What Not To Lead With

- broad Vietnam investment storytelling
- generic company brochure
- too many future parks
- weak or stale listings

## What To Lead With

- verified ready-built factories
- established parks and real land availability
- cluster logic
- site-visit readiness
- commercial clarity

## Recommended 90-Day China Plan

### Days 1-14

- create Chinese deck
- create Chinese landing page copy
- create opportunity brief template
- create first 50 named China/Taiwan target accounts

### Days 15-30

- contact Chinese and Taiwanese business associations in Vietnam
- activate Hai Phong local Chinese business network
- send first 20 tailored introductions or outreach messages
- prepare top 10 verified Hai Phong opportunities

### Days 31-60

- run calls and qualification
- schedule site visits
- refine shortlist packs
- identify 3-5 strongest Chinese market stories

### Days 61-90

- push for first site visits
- push for first negotiation
- document objections and pattern-match demand
- decide whether to add WeChat / Baidu / China-native partner execution

## Inference Notes

- The recommendation to de-prioritize Douyin and WeChat as the first China acquisition engine is an inference from channel fit, your current operating constraints, and the long-cycle B2B nature of industrial deals.
- The recommendation to prioritize associations, account lists, and referrals is an inference from the trust-heavy nature of industrial investment decisions plus the structure of available public networks.
