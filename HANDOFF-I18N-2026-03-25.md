# MACLAND I18N Handoff - 2026-03-25

## Muc tieu

Xay dung va QA ban da ngon ngu cho website MACLAND theo thu tu:

1. English
2. Chinese
3. Korean
4. Japanese

Yeu cau nghiep vu da chot:

- Co nut switch ngon ngu tren head menu.
- Co popup cho user chon ngon ngu o lan truy cap dau tien.
- Cac lan sau ghi nho lua chon truoc do.
- Hien tai dang luu theo cookie trinh duyet.
- IP/header chi duoc dung de de xuat ngon ngu mac dinh lan dau.

## Trang thai hien tai

Da hoan thanh phan nen i18n dau tien:

- Them dictionary cho `vi`, `en`, `zh`, `ko`, `ja`
- Them language switcher trong header desktop/mobile
- Them popup chon ngon ngu lan dau
- Them co che detect locale tu:
  - cookie `macland-lang`
  - `x-vercel-ip-country` / `cf-ipcountry` / `accept-language`
- Da noi dictionary vao:
  - header
  - footer
  - homepage
  - services page
  - about page
  - contact page
  - product listing
  - product detail
  - contact form
  - chatbot

Da verify ky thuat:

- `npm run lint` OK
- `npm run build` OK

Cap nhat bo sung:

- Da them co che `product-translations` rieng de title/content body co the doi theo locale.
- Ban dich hien dang la kieu overlay:
  - neu co translation thi render translation
  - neu chua co thi fallback ve tieng Viet
- Da localize du lieu theo `en/zh/ko/ja` cho:
  - toan bo `72` san pham Hai Phong theo heuristic nghiep vu da siet lai
  - 1 product detail day du ngoai Hai Phong: `khu-cong-nghiep-song-than-1-binh-duong`
- Trong batch Hai Phong da bo sung ca:
  - `title`
  - `content.description`
  - `content.full_content`
  - `location.address`

## Ghi chu quan trong

- Ban dich hien tai la lop UI co cau truc va van hanh duoc.
- Noi dung du lieu san pham trong `public/data.json` van la nguon tieng Viet.
- Nghia la:
  - label giao dien da da ngon ngu
  - mot phan product content da duoc overlay translation
  - nhung phan con lai van fallback ve tieng Viet
- Nen coi day la dang o Phase 2 dang trien khai, chua hoan tat localized content toan bo.
